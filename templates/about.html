About THRESHOLD

We make clothing for the day you're actually having — not the one on a mood board.

THRESHOLD started as a small studio experiment: could a handful of well-made pieces replace a closet full of things that don't quite fit right? Every garment we release is developed over several fittings, cut in small batches, and tested for how it holds up after the twentieth wash, not just the first.

We work with mill partners who share fabric composition and origin on request, and we'd rather ship a smaller collection on time than rush something we're not proud of. If a piece doesn't earn a place in regular rotation, we don't make it again.

What we stand for

  • Fewer, better pieces. We'd rather you buy one thing you wear fifty times.
  • Honest pricing. Our markup is disclosed in our policies page.
  • Easy to return. If it doesn't fit right, sending it back shouldn't be a fight.

Get in touch

Questions about fit, fabric, or an order? Reach us at support@threshold-clothing.example or visit our contact page.
