Policies

Returns & Refunds

  • Unworn items with tags attached can be returned within 7 days of delivery.
  • Start a return by emailing support@threshold-clothing.example with your order number.
  • Once we receive and inspect the item, refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5–7 business days.
  • Cash on Delivery orders are refunded via bank transfer or store credit, your choice.
  • Sale items marked "final sale" cannot be returned, but can still be exchanged for a different size.
  • Return shipping is free for defective or incorrect items; otherwise a flat ₹99 pickup fee applies.

Shipping

  • Orders over ₹1,999 ship free. Orders below that carry a flat ₹99 shipping fee.
  • Standard delivery takes 2–4 business days in metro cities, 4–7 days elsewhere in India.
  • You'll receive a tracking link by email once your order is dispatched.

Sizing & Exchanges

  • Each product page lists true-to-size measurements — check the description before ordering.
  • Wrong size? Exchanges are free within 7 days, subject to stock availability.

Pricing

  • All prices are listed in Indian Rupees (₹) and are inclusive of GST.
  • Struck-through prices show the MRP; the price beside it is what you pay.
  • We don't run flash sales that undercut recent buyers — if a price drops within 7 days of your purchase, we'll refund the difference as store credit.

Privacy

We collect only what's needed to fulfill your order: name, contact details, and shipping address. We do not sell customer data to third parties. Payment details are handled by our payment processor and are never stored on our servers.

Terms of Use

This is a demonstration storefront built for learning purposes. Product images are placeholders, and no real payments are processed. Feel free to adapt the code for your own project.
